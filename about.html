关于酷界国际About Cool World International

酷界国际秉承"让跨境贸易更简单"的愿景，通过强大的技术支撑，致力于为客户降低综合物流运营成本，以"客户优先"的服务理念提供订舱无忧、时效保证、成本可控的跨境物流整体解决方案。

Guided by the vision "making cross-border trade simpler", Cool World uses strong technology to cut overall logistics costs, delivering worry-free booking, guaranteed transit times and cost-controlled solutions with a customer-first service philosophy.

酷界国际是一家专注于国际市场的综合性服务供应链管理公司，是一家中国 MOC 和美国 FMC 注册无船承运人。以厦门为基础，服务延伸至福州、深圳、宁波、上海、青岛等地，同时在越南设有海外公司。

Cool World International is a comprehensive supply chain management company focused on international markets, registered as an NVOCC with China MOC and the US FMC. From our Xiamen base we cover Fuzhou, Shenzhen, Ningbo, Shanghai and Qingdao, with overseas companies in Vietnam and Malaysia.

服务涵盖海运、空运、铁路、快递、报关、仓储、FBA 供应链等，并拥有 Maston、COSCO、EMC、OOCL、ZIM、WHL、YML、ONE、HMM 船司合约。酷界国际期待与您携手并进，互利共赢！

Our services span ocean, air, rail, courier, customs brokerage, warehousing and FBA supply chain, backed by contracts with Maston, COSCO, EMC, OOCL, ZIM, WHL, YML, ONE and HMM. We look forward to moving forward together with you.

愿景与使命Vision & Mission

让跨境贸易更简单Making Cross-border Trade Simpler

以降低客户的运输成本为目标，用强大的技术支持我们的团队提供高效率、高质量的"客户至上"服务，确保低成本、有保证的空间和快速的运输时间，并坚持求真、务实、努力的信念，实现可持续发展。

We aim to lower our customers' shipping costs. Backed by strong technology, our team delivers efficient, high-quality customer-first service — low cost, guaranteed space and fast transit times — with a pragmatic, truth-seeking commitment to sustainable growth.

Gratitude

酷界国际的每个人都有一颗感恩的心。无论是与客户沟通的每一个小细节还是日常与同事互动，只有心存感激、思考别人的想法，才能最大限度减少矛盾，营造和谐轻松的工作氛围。

Everyone at Cool World carries a grateful heart. In every client conversation and everyday interaction, gratitude and empathy minimise friction and build a relaxed, harmonious workplace.

真诚

Sincerity

企业精神：尊重他人，奉献事业。企业理念：客户、员工、企业共同发展。企业宗旨：诚信为本，服务至上。服务标准：专业、高效、创新、满意。

Spirit: respect others, devote ourselves to the work. Philosophy: customers, employees and the company grow together. Purpose: integrity first, service supreme. Standard: professional, efficient, innovative, satisfying.

责任

Responsibility

酷界的文化是一种高度责任感的文化。在发展业务的同时不忘回馈社会，致力于社会公益活动，设立内部救助基金，肩负企业社会责任。

Cool World's culture is one of high responsibility. While growing the business we give back — supporting public welfare and maintaining an internal relief fund as part of our social responsibility.
