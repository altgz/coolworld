From FCL/LCL ocean freight to e-commerce FBA first-mile — six service lines covering the entire international trade chain.

Ocean freight is Cool World's main business. Through win-win cooperation we work with Maston, COSCO, EMC, OOCL, ZIM, WHL, YML, ONE, HMM and more. Our systems connect directly to carrier networks, making operations more accurate, specific, faster and more convenient.

仓储服务Warehousing ServiceDịch vụ kho bãi

Warehousing Service

在仓储管理方面，酷界国际为客户提供便捷的仓储服务，包括本地仓储和海外仓储，此外还提供货物包装和打托盘、贴标等增值服务，让货物在途与在库都清晰可控。

Cool World provides convenient warehousing with both local and overseas facilities, plus value-added services including packaging, palletising and labelling — keeping cargo visible and in control in transit and in storage.

Về quản lý kho bãi, Cool World cung cấp cho khách hàng dịch vụ kho bãi tiện lợi, bao gồm kho nội địa và kho nước ngoài, đồng thời cung cấp các dịch vụ gia trị như đóng gói hàng hóa, xếp pallet và dán nhãn, giúp hàng hóa luôn rõ ràng và kiểm soát được trong quá trình vận chuyển và lưu kho.

Railway Service

Beyond ocean freight, Cool World also operates railway cargo services — providing fast, convenient solutions in a short time for customers who cannot use ocean and need land transport.
